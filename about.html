About

One screen for the global money-and-economy machine — always in motion. Lines are filed Street-style:

  1. Liquidity — Fed plumbing, money markets, credit
  2. Rates — curve, real yields, BEIs, global 10ys
  3. FX — dollar and major pairs
  4. Economy — labor, growth, housing
  5. Inflation — CPI, PCE, expectations
  6. Conditions — vol, credit, financial conditions
  7. Markets — equities, commodities, crypto

Lights score the causal regime (liquidity → transmission → growth/inflation → risk). Equities, commodities, and crypto live under Markets (dollar under FX) — read them as outputs, not inputs. No news feed. No invented numbers.

Glossary

Percentile
Where today’s level sits in the last 1 / 2 / 5 years. 90th = higher than ~90% of readings in that window.
z-score
How many standard deviations today’s level is from the average over that same window. +1 = one σ above average.
1 · 2 · 5
Years in the comparison window for lights, table, and charts. Default 2.
Lights
Five regime dials (liquidity → transmission → growth → inflation → risk). Tap for who voted.
Asset (central bank)
Something the bank owns on its balance sheet — usually bonds, sometimes ETFs or loans — not “stable value.”
Reserves
Cash banks hold at the Fed. Fuel for money markets.
TGA
Treasury General Account — the government’s checking account at the Fed. When it fills, cash leaves banks.
ON RRP
Overnight reverse repo — cash parked at the Fed overnight. Rise drains market liquidity.
Net liquidity
Street shorthand: Fed assets − TGA − ON RRP. Rising = more cash in markets.
Real yield
Yield after inflation (TIPS). Higher real yields = tighter for risk assets.
Breakeven
Market’s implied inflation from nominal vs TIPS yields.
OAS
Option-adjusted spread — extra yield a corporate bond pays over Treasuries. Wider = more credit stress.
VIX / MOVE
Implied volatility — equity fear (VIX) and Treasury fear (MOVE).
Curve (2s10s)
Long yield minus short yield. Inverted (negative) often warns of slowdown.
Outputs
Equities, commodities, crypto, gold — read them under Markets; they don’t drive the lights.
